# Hiring

The loop I use to hire engineers. It comes from two sustained hiring loops: ~36 candidates across ~45 interview rounds, 9 hires, and every offer accepted. I ran hiring together with HR from 2021, from screening through offer, and I wrote the candidate assessments myself. I also co-designed the company's interview loop with a group of managers.

The loop and rubric below are written fresh as a generic template.

## Principles

1. **Decide on evidence, in writing.** Every interviewer writes their assessment before the debrief, so nobody is anchored by the loudest voice.
2. **Test the job.** Use realistic problems from the work the person will do, not puzzles.
3. **Same loop, same rubric.** Candidates for the same role get the same stages and the same scorecard.
4. **Hire for the next level of scope**, not only for today's task list.
5. **Sell honestly.** Candidates are also evaluating us. Tell them the real constraints.

## The loop

| Stage | Who | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Screen | Recruiter / HR | 30 min | Motivation, logistics, communication, compensation range |
| 2. Hiring manager call | Hiring manager | 45 min | Scope, career goals, team fit, what the person wants next |
| 3. Technical deep dive | Senior engineer | 60 min | Walk through a real past project: decisions, trade-offs, failures |
| 4. Practical exercise | Senior engineer | 60-90 min | Live problem or code review in the team's stack |
| 5. Collaboration and values | A peer from another function | 45 min | How they work with product, QA and ops |
| 6. Debrief | All interviewers | 30 min | Written scorecards compared, decision made |
| 7. Offer and references | Hiring manager and HR | - | Offer built from the scorecard and the leveling guide |

Keep the loop to a handful of stages. Every extra round costs candidates and costs you good people.

## Scorecard

Rate each dimension from 1 to 4, and attach one sentence of evidence for each rating. A rating without evidence does not count.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Clear concern; evidence of a gap that matters for the role |
| 2 | Below the bar; could grow, but not within a reasonable time |
| 3 | Meets the bar for the level |
| 4 | Clearly exceeds the bar; strong, specific evidence |

See [`rubric.md`](rubric.md) for the dimensions and what each score looks like.

## Debrief rules

- Scorecards are submitted **before** the debrief.
- Discuss disagreements by pointing at evidence, not at impressions.
- The hiring manager decides. If the decision goes against a strong "no" from an interviewer, write down why.
- "Not sure" means no. Do not hire to fill a seat.

## Candidate experience

- Reply within 2 working days at every stage.
- Give a decision within 3 working days of the final interview.
- Send rejected candidates a short, specific note.

## After the hire

Probation is a check on the hiring decision, not a formality. Set explicit goals at day 1 (see [`../onboarding/30-60-90.md`](../onboarding/30-60-90.md)) and review them honestly. Across the full hire-to-exit cycle, 2 probationary hires did not convert; giving that feedback early was fairer to them and to the team.
